# Engineering Leadership Meeting - 15 September 2026

Attendees: engineering managers of the Platform, Data and Mobile teams.

## Decisions

1. The main application database will be migrated to PostgreSQL 17. The migration must be completed by the end of Q1 2027.
2. Release 4.2 of the customer portal is scheduled for 3 November 2026.
3. A code freeze applies from 15 December 2026 to 6 January 2027. Only critical security fixes may be deployed during the freeze.
4. As a pilot, the Platform team may work remotely four days per week from October to December 2026. The results of the pilot will be reviewed in January 2027.

## Action items

- Data team: prepare the PostgreSQL migration plan by 15 October 2026.
- Mobile team: reduce the app crash rate below 0.5 percent before release 4.2.
