# IT Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and all cloud applications.
- Passwords are not changed on a fixed schedule; they must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected.
- A password manager approved by IT must be used to store work credentials.

## Devices

- All company laptops use full-disk encryption.
- USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Files must be shared through the company file-sharing platform.
- Screens must be locked whenever a device is left unattended.

## Network access

- The company VPN must be used on any network outside the office, including home and public Wi-Fi.

## Reporting incidents

Suspected security incidents, including phishing emails, must be reported to security@example.com within one hour of discovery. Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues. Use the "Report phishing" button in the mail client instead.

## Data classification

Documents are classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Strictly Confidential. Strictly Confidential documents may only be shared with named individuals and must not be stored on personal devices.
