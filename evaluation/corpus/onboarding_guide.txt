New Employee Onboarding Guide

Before your first day
You will receive your laptop by courier before your start date. Please complete the personal data form in the HR portal at least five days before you start.

Your first day
Your first day begins at 09:00 with a welcome session run by HR. In the afternoon you will meet your team and your onboarding buddy. The buddy is an experienced colleague from another team who answers practical questions during your first three months.

Mandatory training
All new employees must complete the security awareness training within 14 days of their start date. The data protection training must be completed within 30 days.

Probation and reviews
The probation period is six months. You will have a review meeting with your line manager after 90 days, and a final probation review before the end of the sixth month.
