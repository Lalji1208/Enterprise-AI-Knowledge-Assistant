Vendor Management FAQ

How long does vendor onboarding take?
Onboarding a new vendor takes about 10 business days after all documents have been received.

Which documents does a new vendor need to provide?
New vendors must provide a signed contract, a completed tax form and proof of insurance.

Who approves new vendors?
New vendors are approved by the procurement team. Contracts with a value above 50,000 EUR also require approval from the legal department.

How are vendor invoices paid?
Vendor invoices are paid within 45 days of the invoice date.
